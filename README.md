# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Project

##  Project Overview
This project analyzes **coffee shop sales data** to uncover insights related to **sales performance, customer footfall, product demand, order patterns, and store location performance**.  
An **interactive Excel dashboard** was created to help business stakeholders make **data-driven decisions**.

---

##  Objectives
- Analyze total sales and customer footfall  
- Identify peak order hours  
- Understand product category & size distribution  
- Compare store location performance  
- Find top-selling products  
- Analyze weekday order trends  

---

##  Key KPIs
- **Total Sales:** $698,812.33  
- **Total Footfall:** 149,116 customers  
- **Average Bill per Person:** 4.69  
- **Average Orders per Person:** 1.4  

---

## Dashboard Insights

### Quantity Orders by Hour
- Orders peak during **morning hours (7–10 AM)**  
- Gradual decline after evening  

###  Category-wise Sales Distribution
- **Coffee** is the highest contributing category  
- Bakery and branded items add secondary revenue  

### Size Distribution of Orders
- **Large & Regular sizes** dominate orders  
- Small size has the lowest share  

###  Store Location Performance
- **Hell’s Kitchen** and **Lower Manhattan** generate the highest sales  
- **Astoria** has lower footfall comparatively  

###  Top 5 Products by Sales
- Barista Espresso  
- Brewed Black Tea  
- Brewed Chai Tea  
- Gourmet Brewed Coffee  
- Hot Chocolate  

### Orders by Weekdays
- Highest orders on **Thursday & Friday**  
- Lower order volume on weekends
